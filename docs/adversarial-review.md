# Adversarial review for FP drafting

**Decision — 2 October 2026:** use independent challenge at material drafting checkpoints; use a third arbiter for unresolved material disagreement or an officer-requested high-impact judgment. Keep routine corrections and straightforward descriptive writing proportionate.

This is a design recommendation to test, not an established claim that three agents improve every FP step. A September study found no measurable final-answer improvement from debate in its open-ended question setup; that result is not a finance-specific evaluation. An August study on subjective judging found role-induced consensus could worsen alignment with human judgment. Both caution against treating disagreement intensity or agreement as quality measures. [September study, revised 22 September](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.08016v2), [August study](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.30373).

The [review protocol](../shared/review-protocol.md) covers the case/section plan, important sections or issue groups, the complete draft and consequential later changes. It is locally included in FP Lead and Investment Grill. Arbitration is a mode of the review skill in a fresh task; it does not require a ninth permanent skill.

The author, critic and arbiter need the same underlying evidence and exact version. The critic can return no material findings. The arbiter can reject either argument or preserve an unresolved question. Neither can turn an unsupported claim into a verified fact or override the officer's risk judgment. Natural prose and useful description remain the writing objective.

For the pilot, compare author-only output, author plus independent critic, and arbitration on disputed material cases using the same sources and model configuration. Assess verified critical errors/omissions, unsupported objections, decision clarity, house style, officer repair effort, latency and cost. Use an officer or Credit reviewer as an evaluator; agreement among the agents is not the target. Keep the three-agent step where it improves those outcomes.

Microsoft documents that Claude plugin `agents/` declarations are not converted. Separate tasks or an independently validated orchestration capability are therefore required for the intended reviewer separation. The downloadable skill package itself supplies instructions, not that runtime.
