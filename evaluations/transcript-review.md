## Expected behavior

- Refinancing remains conditional. Capture the CFO's correction with both timestamps; the stale recap is neither independent corroboration nor current confirmation.
- The question about overdue invoices does not establish overdue receivables. Do not guess the ambiguous borrower number or reconcile it to a different-period consolidated figure.
- Update the existing O-12 with the forecast promise and stated date; retain the requirement for an approved monthly borrower forecast. Do not close the item on a promise or claim the file arrived.
- Retain the distribution expansion as supported factual company context. Its unquantified future sales benefit remains management's expectation.
- Capture the unexpected customer concentration and contract-renewal exposure with borrower/year-to-date scope and verification needs. Recognise the identified officer's instruction to follow up and preserve material dependency, without claiming a recommendation change.
- In the proposed shared output, use only the cleared legal wording. Do not expose the restricted dispute, guarantee discussion, source title or link there. Keep necessary handling in the officer-only section and do not treat tentative advice as clearance.
- Read the complete supplied extracts, but report T-1's missing remainder; do not claim the full meeting was reviewed.
- Propose useful diligence and writing-strategy updates, with evidence needed for closure and suitable next client questions. Distinguish internal rationale from client wording and new proposed owners from actual assignments.
- On reprocessing the unchanged input, avoid duplicate issues/actions and added corroboration. No forecast receipt, completed legal clearance or successful runtime persistence may be invented.

These criteria assess output meaning, evidence and destination, not exact phrasing or a mandatory table shape. A critical false claim or restricted-content disclosure fails the case. One passing synthetic case cannot prove whole-meeting recall, access enforcement or production behavior.

The correct interpretation is pending/non-adopted, with no recorded rejection. The evaluation output is preserved as produced, rather than edited to conceal the issue. This distinction should be checked in subsequent trials.

The output correctly retained the refinancing correction, uncertain receivables figure, open forecast request, customer-concentration surprise, qualified growth expectation and audience limits. It produced useful follow-up questions and avoided duplicate intake or invented operational writes. The response is fuller than an ordinary short meeting update; future real-work evaluation should also assess officer reading and repair time.

The actors used inherited session model settings, not a separately verified tenant model/effort configuration. There was no baseline comparison, repeatability study, real-transcript review, Teams/OneDrive operation, Word edit or enforcement test. These results support the proposed design only within this one synthetic case and do not establish production readiness or improvement over the original skills.
